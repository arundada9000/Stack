Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 July 2026

This page explains how the Stack Visualizer at easystack.netlify.app handles information. The site is a free educational tool built and maintained by Arun Neupane. In short: the visualizer runs entirely in your browser, and we do not collect or store your personal data on any server.

What happens in your browser

  • Stack operations: every push, pop and peek happens locally on your device. The values you enter are never uploaded anywhere and disappear when you close the page.
  • Code snippets: the copyable code examples are static text served with the page. Copying them uses your browser's clipboard only.
  • Syntax highlighting: the code display uses the Prism library loaded from the Cloudflare CDN. When your browser requests those files, the CDN receives your IP address as part of a standard web request, as with any website resource.
